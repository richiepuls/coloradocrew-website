About the Club

Where the Rockies
Meet the Water

Colorado Crew is a club rowing team founded in 1992 that strives to encourage and push the limits of its members through practice and competition. It is the ultimate team sport, with every individual working together toward the same goal.

We practice at the Boulder Reservoir and travel across the country to compete. Strong bonds, lasting memories, and open to all experience levels.

Rent-a-Rower Fundraising Program

Crew members available for hire for odd jobs that help fund team dues. Reach out to crewjobs@colorado.edu.
